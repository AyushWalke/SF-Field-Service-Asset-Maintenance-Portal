# List Views

## Overview

The FSAM Portal includes business-oriented list views that provide users with quick access to frequently used records without requiring reports.

---

## Customer Assets

### Active Assets

Displays customer assets that are currently active.

### Warranty Expiring Soon

Shows assets with active warranties approaching expiration.

---

## Service Requests

### Open Service Requests

Displays all active service requests requiring attention.

### High Priority Requests

Displays high and critical priority service requests.

### Completed Requests

Provides quick access to completed service requests.

---

## Maintenance Visits

### Upcoming Visits

Shows scheduled maintenance visits.

### In Progress Visits

Displays maintenance visits currently being performed.

### Completed Visits

Displays completed maintenance visits.

---

## Service Parts

### Active Parts

Displays all active service parts.

### Low Stock Parts

Identifies service parts that require replenishment.

---

## Design Considerations

- List views are optimized for common operational workflows.
- Filters reduce unnecessary navigation for end users.
- Sorting prioritizes the most relevant records.
- Visibility is configured for all users to support team collaboration.
