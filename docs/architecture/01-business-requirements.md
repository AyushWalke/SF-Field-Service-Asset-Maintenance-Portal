# Business Requirements

## Project

**Field Service Asset Maintenance Portal (FSAM)**

## Business Requirements

  ID      Requirement
  ------- ------------------------------------------------------
  BR-01   Customers can own multiple assets.
  BR-02   Every asset has a unique serial number.
  BR-03   Support agents can register customer assets.
  BR-04   Customers can raise service requests for an asset.
  BR-05   Each request belongs to exactly one asset.
  BR-06   Managers assign service engineers.
  BR-07   Engineers update job progress.
  BR-08   Engineers record maintenance visits.
  BR-09   Engineers can record replaced parts.
  BR-10   Managers monitor SLA compliance.
  BR-11   Complete maintenance history is retained.
  BR-12   Reports and dashboards provide operational insights.
